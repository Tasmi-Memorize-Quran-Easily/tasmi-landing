How to use TestFlight

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone (free, made by Apple).
  2. Open this page on your iPhone and tap the Join the beta on TestFlight button above.
  3. Tap Accept in TestFlight to join the Tasmi beta.
  4. Tap Install in TestFlight to download Tasmi. It will appear on your home screen with an orange dot next to its name.
  5. Launch Tasmi and sign in with your Quran Foundation account to begin.

If anything doesn’t work, reach us directly at israasaifullah@gmail.com. We’ll respond within a few hours, in shaa Allah.
