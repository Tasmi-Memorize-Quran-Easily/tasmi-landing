How to use TestFlight

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone (free, made by Apple).
  2. Submit the form above with the Apple ID email signed into your iPhone.
  3. Check your email for an invitation from Apple titled “You’re invited to test Tasmi”.
  4. Open the email on your iPhone and tap View in TestFlight (or Accept).
  5. Tap Install in TestFlight to download Tasmi. It will appear on your home screen with an orange dot next to its name.
  6. Launch Tasmi and sign in with your Quran Foundation account to begin.

If anything doesn’t work, reach us directly at israasaifullah@gmail.com. We’ll respond within a few hours, in shaa Allah.
