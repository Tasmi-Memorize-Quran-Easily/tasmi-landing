FEATURES

What Tasmi does

Seven-stage challenges

A new portion moves through structured stages, from first reading to confident recall. Each stage is short, focused, and self-directed.

Synced across devices

Progress, bookmarks, and streaks follow your Quran Foundation account, so you can pick up where you left off on any device.

Listen along

Multi-reciter audio for every verse, with adjustable repeat and pace. Use it as a passive driver or as an active practice partner.

Spaced review

Once memorized, portions return at increasing intervals so they don’t fade. The schedule adapts to how well you recall.

Private by design

No audio recording. No advertising identifiers. No third-party analytics. Your memorization is between you and your Lord.

Daily streaks

A gentle nudge, not a guilt trip. Streaks track your consistency without turning memorization into a game.
